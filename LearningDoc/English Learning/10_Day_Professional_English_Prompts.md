# 10-Day Professional English Plan (Prompt List)

**Goal:** Manager ane senior management sathe email ane conversation ma politely, confidently ane professionally vaat kari shaku, mari vaat defend kari shaku ane respectful rite argue kari shaku — embarrass thaya vagar.

---

## How to use this file (Kevi rite vaparvu)

1. Roj **ek j prompt** copy karo ane Claude ne paste karo.
2. Claude tamne ek `.md` file banavi ne aapshe — te download karo.
3. Te divase file **2 vaar moteth (out loud) vancho**, ane highlight karela phrases memorize karo.
4. Raat ma 5 minute: aaje shikhela 5 phrases notebook ma lakho ane ek vaar bolo.
5. Biji divase navo prompt aapta pehla, aagla divas na 5 phrases yaad karo (revision).

> Tip: Prompt ma `[ ]` brackets ma je lakhyu chhe te tamari job pramane badli shako chho (example: tamaro role, industry). Na badlo to pan chalshe.

---

## 10-Day Overview

| Day | Topic | Tame shu shikhsho |
|-----|-------|-------------------|
| 1 | Professional email basics | Email structure, polite opening/closing, status update |
| 2 | Requests & follow-ups | Leave, approval, resources maangva; reminder moklva bina rude thaya |
| 3 | Polite disagreement | Manager ni vaat thi asahmat thai ne respectful rite potani vaat mukvi |
| 4 | Defending your work | Blame aave tyare calmly facts sathe potane defend karvu |
| 5 | Saying no & pushing back | Vadhare kaam ke impossible deadline ne politely "no" kehvu |
| 6 | Owning mistakes | Bhool sweekarvi, over-apology vagar, solution sathe |
| 7 | Meetings with senior management | Idea present karvo, interrupt karvu, tough questions handle karva |
| 8 | Escalation & conflicts | Biji team/colleague sathe problem professionally escalate karvi |
| 9 | Negotiation | Appraisal, salary hike, promotion ane recognition maate vaat karvi |
| 10 | Final revision & mock test | Mixed difficult scenarios, role-play ane self-test |

## Bonus Prompts (10 divas pachhi practice maate)

### Live role-play practice

```
Act as my strict manager. Scenario: [e.g. you are unhappy that my project is delayed]. Talk to me one message at a time and wait for my reply. After 6–8 exchanges, stop and give me feedback: what sounded professional, what sounded weak or rude, and better phrases I could have used. End with a vocabulary table: Word/Phrase | Simple English explanation | Gujarati meaning (in Gujarati script).
```

### Review my real email

```
Here is an email I want to send to my [manager/director]: [paste email]. Rewrite it to sound polite, confident and professional. Then explain in a short table what you changed and why. End with a vocabulary table of new words: Word/Phrase | Simple English explanation | Gujarati meaning (in Gujarati script).
```

### Quick daily phrase dose

```
Give me 10 new professional English phrases for [situation, e.g. replying to a senior who is angry]. For each: the phrase, when to use it, one example sentence, and the Gujarati meaning (in Gujarati script).
```
